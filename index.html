Smart Railway Safety System

Making railway platforms safer with automatic safety barriers, smart sensors and a movable walkway.

01. Problem Identification

Passengers face serious risks while boarding and exiting trains. Accidental falls onto railway tracks and gaps between trains and platforms can cause accidents. A safer boarding system is needed.

02. Our Main Solution

Automatic Safety Barrier

Protective doors separate passengers from railway tracks. They open automatically when the train stops at the correct position.

Movable Walkway

An adjustable walkway extends between the train entrance and platform, replacing the need to step across the gap or use conventional stairs.

Smart Automatic Operation

Sensors coordinate the barrier doors and movable walkway to improve passenger safety.

03. Smart Sensor Technology

Train Detection Sensor

Identifies the approaching train.

Position Sensor

Checks whether the train has stopped at the correct position.

Obstacle Detection Sensor

Checks whether passengers or objects are blocking the doors. Closing is stopped if an obstacle is detected.

Gap Detection Sensor

Measures the gap between the train and platform to help adjust the movable walkway.

04. Animated Working Demonstration

Watch the proposed safety barrier and walkway system.

System Ready

How It Works

1. Train detection sensor identifies the arriving train.
2. Position sensor checks the train's stopping position.
3. Safety barrier doors open automatically.
4. Gap sensor helps adjust the movable walkway.
5. Passengers board and exit safely.
6. Obstacle sensor checks that the passage is clear.
7. Walkway retracts and barrier doors close.

05. Additional Feature - Electricity Generation

Energy from Door Movement

A small generator connected to the barrier door mechanism can convert some mechanical energy from door movement into electrical energy.

⚙️

Generator Ready

The generated electricity could be stored in a small battery and used for low-power applications. Actual energy output needs practical testing.

06. Expected Benefits

Passenger safety and protection from accidental falls.
Easy boarding and exiting through the movable walkway.
Better operation using smart sensors.
Additional possibility of energy recovery.

07. Conclusion

RailSafe aims to improve railway passenger safety through automatic safety barriers, smart sensors and a movable walkway. Electricity generation is an additional feature that may recover some energy from door movement.
